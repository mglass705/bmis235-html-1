J.P. Morgan Headquarters, NYC

270 Park Ave. New York, NY 10017

The JPMorganChase Tower is a 60-story skyscraper in New York City and the tallest all-electric tower in the city. From the street it appears to float above the ground, because giant fan-shaped columns lift the entire building over active underground train tracks..

Earth is about 4.5 billion years old. It spins once a day, circles the Sun once a year, and carries about 8 billion people along for the ride.

Three facts about The J.P. Morgan Tower

  • The tower stands 1,388 feet tall and has 60 stories.
  • It runs entirely on electricity powered by renewable hydroelectric energy.
  • About 97% of the previous building's demolition materials were recycled, reused, or upcycled.
